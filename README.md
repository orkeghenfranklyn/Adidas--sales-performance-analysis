# Adidas Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

How can sales data help a business understand its performance and make better decisions?

This project analyses Adidas US sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue, operating profit, units sold, and performance across product categories, regions, and retailers.

The project transforms raw sales records into summarised metrics, comparative visualisations, and actionable business recommendations through an Excel-based analysis and dashboard.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales and operating profit?
- Which regions contribute the most to total sales?
- Which retailers record the highest sales revenue?
- What key performance indicators help summarise overall business performance?
- What actions could the business consider based on the findings?

## Tools and Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data review, calculations, analysis, and reporting
- **Excel formulas and functions:** KPI calculations and aggregation
- **PivotTables:** Comparison of product, regional, and retailer performance
- **Charts:** Visual comparison of sales and operating profit
- **Dashboard design:** Presentation of key metrics and business findings

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Review and Quality Checks
Reviewed the original dataset and checked for duplicate records, blank cells, and missing values. The checks performed did not identify duplicates or blanks requiring correction.

### 2. KPI Analysis
Calculated the following key performance indicators:

- Total Revenue
- Total Operating Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Operating Margin

### 3. Performance Analysis
Used PivotTables to compare performance across product categories, sales regions, and retailers.

### 4. Dashboard Development
Built an Excel dashboard combining KPI summaries, charts, key findings, and business recommendations in one view.

### 5. Documentation
Documented the project objective, tools, data checks, analytical findings, and recommendations within the workbook.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified the following patterns in the dataset:

- **Product performance:** Men's Street Footwear recorded the highest sales and operating profit among the product categories analysed.
- **Regional performance:** The West region recorded the highest sales revenue.
- **Retailer performance:** West Gear recorded the highest sales revenue among the retailers analysed.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the business could consider the following actions:

- **Product strategy:** Review demand and profitability patterns for Men's Street Footwear when planning inventory and product allocation.
- **Regional strategy:** Investigate the West region's performance to identify practices that may be useful in other regions.
- **Retailer strategy:** Examine West Gear's sales contribution and explore opportunities to strengthen the commercial relationship.

These recommendations are starting points for further investigation; the analysis identifies performance patterns but does not establish their underlying causes.
